
# Airline Performance Dashboard

An interactive dashboard that looks at domestic flight prices and travel times across 6 airlines and 6 Indian cities (Bangalore, Kolkata, Chennai, Mumbai, Hyderabad and Delhi). It is built on 200K+ flight records.

## The problem
Ticket prices change a lot depending on where you fly, how many stops you make and when you travel. I wanted to see which of these factors really move the price, so an airline or a traveller could make better route and pricing decisions.

## What you can explore
- Average price, average duration and flight count as headline numbers
- Average price by destination city
- Average duration by airline and city
- Average price by number of stops, arrival time and departure time
- Filters for airline and travel class (Business or Economy)

## What I found
In the Business class view:
- Destination matters. Bangalore (about 51K), Kolkata and Chennai (about 50K) are the most expensive, while Delhi is the cheapest (about 42K).
- Stops change the price. Nonstop flights average about 25.5K, one stop about 49.2K and two or more stops about 57.4K.
- Time of day matters much less. The six arrival-time groups sit at similar shares (about 16 to 19 percent each).
